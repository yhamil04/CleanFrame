# Monochrome launcher marks

Brand marks identify their respective services in CleanFrame. They are not endorsements. White paths are recoloured from the linked SVGs; size and opacity are controlled by the interface. ABC uses a Lissajous mark from the MIT Custom Brand Icons source; SBS uses the feather extracted from its MIT SBS Chill mark; labels disambiguate the streaming services. No fabricated monograms. Utility outlines are original and share a 24-unit grid; this includes the two input glyphs, `input-tv` (a television with an antenna, for Live TV) and `input-hdmi` (a generic port outline, for HDMI and other external inputs), which depict no third-party mark.

Simple Icons sources are CC0; trademarks remain with their owners. Viaplay, ITVX and Twitch come unchanged from the simple-icons npm package 16.35.0. Pathé and BBC iPlayer have no mark there and keep their initials. Custom Brand Icons retains its MIT licence; this source record does not grant trademark rights. See SOURCES.json for exact revisions and adaptations.

Prime uses the two smile subpaths, not clipped wordmark text. Apple Music uses the note subpath without the outer app tile. These are intentional simplified marks beside full service names. Simple Icons CC0 and Custom Brand Icons MIT texts are included here. The old Australian TV source GPL text is retained for provenance only; no current icon uses that source.
